Connectome canvas

Voxel, Frame, Graph and Gaussian splats of four EM examples, drawn on one section and in 3D. Lengths are in nanometres.

What these results are. A first pass on one small block per dataset, made by render/make_canvas_data.py. The Gaussians are a regular grid fitted to the image with the prototype slab renderer: one seed, scored on the sections they were fitted to, no held-out sections. The Frame is a TEASAR skeleton of the labels, not yet the Gaussians of the design. The full study (held-out sections, Frame-initialised Gaussians, repeats) has not been run.

Section view

Image:
Gaussians: 1σ ellipses of those within one section of the plane; line sheet blob. Frame: circles of the skeleton nodes crossing the section, at their radius. Graph: objects whose centre is within 3 sections, with their contacts.

Scores of the four examples

PSNR against the raw block, in dB, higher is closer. "Next section" is the PSNR between neighbouring raw sections: what a render scores if it only repeats the section beside it. Bytes per voxel counts 11 float32 numbers per Gaussian; the raw image is 1 byte per voxel.
